The Innaugural Aggie Open

One of the largest badminton tournament in Northern California.

For the first time ever, the Badminton Club at UC Davis presents The Aggie Open! A 2-day tournament hosted at Elite Badminton Center in Union City, CA. With HNDRD as our sponsor, we offer unique cash and material prizes, as well as a chance to compete against some of the best players in Northern California. Hosted by seasoned tournament organizers, come participate in a historic event!

Davis-themed trophies, cash prizes and tournament shirts await the winners.
